About Us

Vision: Transform Lives.


Mission: To transform lives through skilling and supporting vulnerable mothers, youth and communities in South Sudan.


LIGHT SOUTH SUDAN is a community-based organization committed to empowering vulnerable communities through education, vocational training, entrepreneurship development, humanitarian assistance and community development initiatives.

Our Programs

Youth Empowerment

Skills training, entrepreneurship, leadership and employment readiness programs.

Women Empowerment

Supporting vulnerable mothers through livelihood and financial literacy programs.

Education Support

Scholarships, school support and community learning initiatives.

Community Development

Water, sanitation, environmental protection and peacebuilding activities.

Current Projects

Youth Skills Development

Training young people in vocational and business skills.

Women's Economic Empowerment

Supporting women with business training and income generation opportunities.

Clean Communities Campaign

Promoting environmental protection and community sanitation.
